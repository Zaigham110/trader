Time machine

Replays the stored gold history, minute by minute, through the same strategy, session rules, risk limits and costs the live system uses. The AI agents are not replayed: asked about a past date, a model remembers what happened next, so its answer would not be honest. Their effect is measured on live decisions only.

Paper test

Strategies being tried on the live market on paper only. Trades here are simulated at the smallest size (0.01 lot) with real spreads; no order is ever sent.

Do the AI vetoes help?

Each time the strategy's rule wanted a trade, this records what happened to it and plays it forward as a code-only trade. R means multiples of the amount risked. If vetoed signals would have lost, the vetoes are worth their cost.

Gold futures positioning

Large speculators' net position in COMEX gold futures (CFTC report). Slow weekly context shown to the Strategy and Risk advisor agents; it is not a timing signal and no code rule uses it.
